# 4 Sentence Summary
The story feels like a modern day James Bond movie. It taps into some of the anxiety of 2026, however felt rushed at the end.

The game is a linear action/stealth game, with semi open world segments.

The difficulty of the game is low, with little variation in combat or skill involved.

I finished the main story on normal difficulty in 16.5 hours.

# Storytelling - James Bond Origin
The story that is told is basically that of IOI's own take on James Bond, the famous spy. The story is serviceable, with a strong start that falls off midway through. It taps into the theme in 2026 (which is AI) and the villain motive is compelling, at the end they are clearly setting up a sequence hook. However the final segment felt very rushed.

# Gameplay - A bit of stealth, action and very little driving

## Stealth

Stealth is what Bond is known for, on top of gadgets to distract potential watchers, Bond is also able to activate distractions. He also has the ability to lure enemies or bluff them, in practice I rarely remember to use these.

There are two type of stealth here
Social Stealth - For these types it is not possible to turn to combat, it usually occurs in the public and if caught Bond just teleports away. Usually for these there are a few solutions which all lead to the same outcome.
Combat Stealth - These are more traditional stealth experiences as the player tries to finish some objective without getting seen. It is reasonably well implemented and the bluffing ability makes it possible to get away with non perfect play. 
Both of them ensure that the game never gets stale during stealth sections as the game switches between both as we go from a social camouflage to sneaking around high security.

## Combat 

Melee combat is what you turn to if stealth fails, there is a parry and a dodge. James Bond also has a grab he can use as well as certain environmental objects. It starts off strong, however Bond never gets any new moves, and the enemy types are limited. So combat becomes boring after a while.

In terms of the shooter element, it is a fairly generic shooting game. One notable thing is that James Bond never carries a lot of ammo, making it necessary to switch weapons.

## Misc
The driving segment is really handholded, if you are able to inconvenience some poor pedestrian the car will auto steer out of their way. Except for a chase near the start, driving never felt exciting as the path is always linear with no possibility of failure.

The bosses are a disappointment and do not take advantage of the game system fully. Only the first boss fight at the basement makes use of Bond's hacking tool extensively, after that the fights fall off into overly gimmicky fights. I don't feel like a spy outsmarting the opponent, I feel like John fucking Wick shooting a continental soldier, where unless you fulfill its gimmick it does nothing.

To discuss the dialogue options I am pretty confident there is 0 variation in what happens. It does not matter what Bond replies things turn out the same. There is only a single moment where you can fail if you didn't choose correctly. It really puts a dent in the mode knowing the dialogue choices do not matter.

Finally I must talk about this, there is a ridiculous amount of handholding. I don't need my mission control to tell me to check out X where X is always the solution. I was trying to solve a puzzle to get past a door and my partner just shows up and tells me to get over. Is it that bad to struggle on a puzzle? If I cannot figure it out let me google it, not have the NPC point it out.

# Non Spoiler summary
I felt that the story is the only reason to play the game, if a James Bond style story isn't for you it is a safe skip. The gameplay is not strong enough to justify the time spent. Tac Sim mode seems promising... but it is quite barebones at the moment.

# Spoiler Discussion

Personally I felt that the game has a very strong start, it starts strong escalating as Bond goes from a regular soldier to getting recruited then to the mission in Slovakia. The car chase feels straight out of a Bond Movie, taking shortcuts to catch up and ending in a badass segment where we get to control the tilt of the plane to get through the baddies. At this point the Slovakia mission has a few options, from burning a bin to swiping an employee key card.

## Railroading
After the above mission, we get to go to a pirates' market. There are several activities possible here, in a full exploration where you need to earn money for the auction. I did the boxing ring, and the art deal. Once I get my goal ALL activities instantly become unavailable. I wanted to do the cup game, or the phone hacking, or the shooting range, the game disagrees and thinks I should go back to Greenway. 

On top of that with exactly one exception, the dialogue options do not matter. You know in Casino Royale where Bond won with trickery and insight? I don't feel like Bond when none of my options matter. The only part where this is implemented is taunting Damien to get him close so we can hack his phone, this is the only smart part. Meanwhile when we have a contact that is blown, or when we are about to be lowered to alligators, regardless what I cannot lose. It takes the tension out.

## Repetitive gameplay
The formula of the game is essentially doing social stealth, then regular stealth, and ending with a shoot out. The enemy does not actually change, the enemies early on down at the laundry bay feel exactly the same as the guards in the penultimate area. The guns can be summarized as pistol, shotgun, assault rifle, submachine gun and a sniper rifle, with suppressed variants. The enemy type can be summarized as normal and armored guys. All in all I felt like I was doing the exact same thing in a loop.
Also the sheer amount of walking or following a character isn't helping, just make it a cutscene if I cannot deviate from the path.

## Lackluster bosses
The only boss fight is the fight against Monroe. Literally the first fight, it is a cool CQC fight spanning across multiple locations. A strong start, however all other bosses aren't that unique.
Tero Murto requires the usage of traps to damage him, however it just ends after 3 hits. The subsequent CQC is completely cut scene based.
Meanwhile the other Murto twin is a standard CQC fight in the same building, yes it gets set on fire later on but the boss himself is unchanged.
The fight against Damien is extremely similar to Tero Murto, where it is completely dependent on the surroundings.
The final boss has a phase 1 which involves the same thing as Tero, while a phase 2 that is basically the Anor Londo archers. It takes place... in the sewers. So the visual sucks, and it is not interesting or difficult. The story should have ended after Antarctica, not only does the final boss NOT use melee combat which the game does well in, it is barely a fight.

## Conclusions
Overall this game is not my thing, it felt tailor-made to the most casual of audiences. It is definitely not worth the almost $70 price tag. The Tac Sim seems to be the intended post game but it is really underbaked. After a year or so maybe it will be worth the price.
