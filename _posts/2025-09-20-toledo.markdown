# Day Trip
## Toledo
Compared to the other day trip I was impressed by the train station upon leaving the train. Even before arriving I can see the river and the library.
I went in and walked around a bit and it was pretty quiet, though it is narrow.
I also walked past a few castle walls and it looks nice.

## Museo del Ejército
There is a lot of exhibits on the military here, I thought the weapons are interesting and how much decorations went into the weapons despite it not helping with functionality. The diorama of the parade is pretty nice.

Next I also visited the upper floors and the castle looks very nice. I saw that people are quite creative in trying to kill each other. I saw a duel pistol as well as an infographic on how chemistry back then was used for gunpowder. Once again I felt that I lack some history to fully appreciate the nuance, but the Muslim influence is surprising. One of the tents has some Muslim inspirations for example.

## Along the Streets
As the city of swords damn there are a lot of swords. I was surprised by the sheer amount of swords that mimic popular characters and series though. Though it is a shame I can't bring one back to my home nation, maybe in the future I will move here instead just to get some swords.

## Don Quixote
There was a statue of Don Quixote in the front of the stairs, I thought it looks pretty neat. I took a picture with it just like a tourist. There are some other interesting things like the cave of Hercules, and the cathedral.

I thought the rivers are nice, it looks clear and the dam has a circular shape. As the sun sets I look at the sunset near the train station, the historical Milis are quite nice. This is one of the cities where I felt that there are still things to do.
