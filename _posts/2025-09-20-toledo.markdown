# Day Trip
## Toledo
Compared to the other day trip I was impressed by the train station upon leaving the train. Even before arrivign I can see the river and the libray.
I went in and walk around a bit and it was pretty quiet, through it is narrow.
I also walked past a few castle walls and it looks nice.

## Museo del Ejército
There is a lot of exhibit on the military here, I thought hte weapons are intresting and how much decorations went into the weapons despite it not helping with functionality. The diorama of the paradeis pretty nice.

Next I also visited the upper floors and the castle looks very nice. I saw that people are quite creative in trying to kill each other. I saw a duel pistol as well as an infographic on how chemistry back then is used for gunpowder. Once again I felt that I lack some history to fully appericate the nunance, but the muslim influence is suprising. One of the tents has some muslim inspirations for example.

## Along the Streets
As the city of swords damn there are a lot of swords. I was suprise by the sheer amount of swords that mimic popular characters and series through. Through it is a shame I can't bring one back to my home nation, maybe in the future I will move here instead just to get some swords.

## Don Quixote
There was a statue of Don Quioxite in the front of the stairs, I thought it looks pretty neat. I took a picture with it just like a tourist. There are some other interesting things like the cave of herclues,and the cathedral.

I thought the rivers are nice, it looks clear and the dam has a circular shape. As the sun sets I look at the sunset near the train station, the historical Milis are quite nice. This is one of the city where I felt that there are still things to do.
