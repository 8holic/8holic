# Day 1 — Arrival, Cup Noodles Museum & Dotonbori

<figure class="post-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/post-photos/2025-05-15-featured.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt=""><figcaption>Osaka, Japan</figcaption></figure>

## Overview

Today is the first day I arrive in Japan. I took the plane at midnight and arrived in the morning. Before heading to my hostel I visited the following location.

## Cup Noodles Museum Osaka Ikeda

### Intent

As a college student who eats a lot of instant noodles to survive for my college exam, I am very familiar with Japanese branded instant noodles. The Cheesy Curry Chicken is my favorite. So when I was looking around and saw a Cup Noodles Museum I decided to visit just to see what is up.

### Experience

The museum is smaller than expected, as most of the things are in Japanese, I did not really understand much of the exhibit. However the making of the instant noodles is worth it.

### Memory

The Instant Noodle making is the highlight, I get to design my own cup and add my own ingredients. Additionally I learnt how instant noodles are prepared. I get to see the lid sealer that melts the lid onto the cup.

### Insight

For small and niche museums such as these the priority is the interactivity and not the exhibits itself. I should look up such activities in the future before visiting.

## Ikeda Castle Ruins

### Intent

It happened to be nearby, and I kinda like castles. So I decided to visit it since it is convenient.

### Experience

There is a park around the castle, at the top floor it leads to an observatory that provides an overview of the more civilian part of the city. The castle has a Stair climbing wheelchair, additionally there are a few small gardens.

### Memory

The sunset is a nice sight (as seen in the picture above). It becomes very quiet the instant I enter the garden, having quiet and peace is nice. Additionally the Stair Climbing Wheelchair attached to this, made me realize how rare this is in my home country.

### Insight

Spontaneous trip that is effortless to visit are worth the efforts, no need to plan rigidly for everything. Additionally I realize the gap in infrastructure in terms of disability access, and this is something worth advocating.

## Dotonbori 

### Intent

It is surprisingly close to the hostel I was staying at so I paid it a visit. I also heard something about the running man.

### Experience

It is really crowded, and yup the running man is there. I ate some nice food and walked around a bit.

### Memory

The food is nice, also I saw Spiderman on the bridge.

### Insight

I realize that I should probably research more about the famous landmarks, since without any knowledge of the historical significance the running man is just another banner.

# Day 2 — Osaka Castle & World Expo

<figure class="post-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/post-photos/2025-05-16-featured.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt=""><figcaption>Osaka, Japan</figcaption></figure>

## Overview

Today was packed with two priorities, I visited the Osaka Castle as well as the World Expo.

## Osaka Castle

### Intent

I was interested in old stuff, so I expect to see an old castle preserved into the modern era and maybe learn some history about it.

### Experience

The Anime Figurine Museum was a surprise which was actually the first thing I saw. As it was part of my ticket I went in and saw figures across the centuries. After buying some snacks from here, I proceeded to the castle and found that it is now a museum basically. I also took a boat ride around the moat of the castle.

### Memory

Regarding the Anime Figurine figure it was interesting to look at the progression of the figurine across time and how it has changed. Additionally there are dioramas of various famous scenes like Iron Man and the fighter jet as well as Godzilla. 

For the castle it was more modern than expected, the interior feels like any other museum. It is English translated and some things are interesting, such as a particular section on Dutch-Japan friendship where I learnt that the Dutch were one of the few that were trading with Japan during the isolation. 

Finally the boat ride I remember seeing the "demon face" statue, where the guide told us that it is supposed to ward off evil, I thought this was interesting to witness these superstitions.

### Insight

The most important lesson from this is definitely time management, I did not research everything and I missed a boat ride as well as one of the towers due to taking a wrong turn. In the future I will either allocate more time or at least look out for time sensitive parts in a region.

## Japan World Expo 2025

### Intent - Overall

The mainstay of the trip, my professor mentioned this event and showed us a video. Since then I have been kinda curious about this event which started back in 1897, and it seems like a good way to learn much about the world. I booked the night ticket expecting it to be less crowded. I have also received a slot at the Japanese Pavilion via the lottery system. I will be splitting this section sequentially based on my arrival.

### Experience - Malaysia & Singapore Pavilion

The Malaysia building represents a wooden structure and the first part is a market. Afterwards there are some sections on the hope of the nation and their smart city plan.

Singapore building is a giant red dot that certainly stands out, the first floor is full of glass trees and similar things.

### Memory - Malaysia & Singapore Pavilion

Malaysia section most memorable section is the entrance where it is about past culture, everything after that feels like generic big city stuff.

Singapore memorable section is the glass section on the first floor, there was one section using Augmented Reality where we can enter our wishes and hopes.

### Insight - Malaysia & Singapore Pavilion

I learnt not to visit surrounding countries as the information isn't new and while visually it is appealing, the content are mostly of things I already know.

### Experience - Japan Pavilion

The Japan Pavilion actually consists of three sections representing a cyclic cycle for a sustainable future. It was also as I learnt the largest wooden structure at that time. I saw a showcase of how biodegradable plastic breaks down in water exhibit, as well as a comic strip of Doraemon of the Ise Jingu. Finally we reach the center 1/3 of the way through and there is a queue for looking at the Narashino Meteorite and one for touching it, obviously I went and touched it and it felt just like any other rock. Afterwards it is focused on the bio reactor and how they produce energy with algae, ending off with DNA strands like decorations with mirrors making it appear like the DNA strands in a human body.

### Memory - Japan Pavilion

There are a lot of interesting things here, so to summarise

- The Ise Jingu and the ceremonic rebuilding every 20 years to ensure that Shinmei-zukuri technique used is preserved across generations as well as the structure of the building and it clearly seems to be working pretty well.

- A showcase of how much more water does it take to raise an animal compared to algae, the ratio is 50:1. It is hard to imagine how much meat consumption affects the planet.

- The central ring was really relaxing and calming, a good waiting spot for the Narashino Meteorite. Even though the entire day was quite packed this moment was one of calm.

- The bio reactor plant where waste water from the expo is channeled right here and is used to create energy. It took a while to understand but once I realize what it is below my feet it was like a miracle.

### Insight - Japan Pavilion

This was one of the best part of the day, the exhibits are exciting. I learnt to stop and contemplate the various exhibitions and stay longer than a glance. 

### Experience - Conclusion

Initially I had planned to visit other pavilions like China and Spain, however I soon realize that the night ticket means that by the time I have left Japan Pavilion most other Pavilion are starting to close their doors. Some of those that are still open are the Brazil Pavilion as well as Bangladesh. Since we are near the end I went to the top and had an overview of the entire place and watched the final drone show before heading back. The queue back is long but the Japanese trains were efficient.

### Memory - Others

The Brazil pavilion left the most impact due to being a shade of pink with plastic representing trees, where the tree seems to grow and inflate. I sat at one of the chairs and it was a strange experience.

The train back is also very long, I think it took 50 minutes for me to leave via the station.

### Insight - Others

One of the things I learnt is to trust my instincts and explore what I am curious about. Additionally I need to do more research before buying tickets as I did not get to visit as many pavilions as I wanted. Finally I learnt how to tolerate long queues.

# Day 4 — Tomogashima Island

## Tomogashima Island

### Intent

As the weather is great, and I am always curious about abandoned places(since they are illegal to visit in Singapore), I decided to pull the trigger for this. I was expecting a hike basically in the wilderness

### Experience

It took 1 hour to get from Osaka to Wakayama, Kada Village. At 9am where the ferry is supposed to leave the weather suddenly worsened causing the ferry to halt. However at 1030am all is good so I went ahead. On the island I visited the observatory and it is pretty nice. I also met up with two nice lads and we became acquainted. At the end we missed the initial ferry back so we waited together for an hour for the 4pm ferry.

### Memory

For a moment I was concerned that the trip needed to be cancelled. The interesting things are the World War 2 fort as well as former shophouses in the era. Additionally there was a statue there.

### Insight

- I learnt from the other two to have a more exposed field of view, and I find it rather meaningful. Paying attention to the rocks for example he can tell that it is recently exposed because the algae growth is lesser compared to others. 

- Additionally talking to other random travellers is a risk worth taking. I am usually rather reserved but it was a really enjoyable experience.

# Day 5 — Ikoma Amusement Park & Nara

<figure class="post-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/post-photos/2025-05-19-featured.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt=""><figcaption>Osaka, Japan</figcaption></figure>

## Ikoma Sanjo Amusement Park

### Intent

An amusement park on the hill was something I was curious about, the main drive is curiosity. 

### Experience

I went up via a train, the amusement park is free to visit. Payment is only for specific rides. I went to the flying plane as well as the "White House". Afterwards I climbed down the hill and walked back to the train.

### Memory

The trains are very cute and have a nice design. The flying plane is great because I was able to see both Nara and Osaka from a high overview. Additionally the White House depicted an interesting version of America, the building is tilted by a few degrees. The founding myth was shown as a bridge, then followed by a steam train. Notably the train noise is clearly a vacuum cleaner. At the end, Lego was used to depict the moon landing.

### Insight

Overall the entire place was really enjoyable despite the low budget vibe. Some of the creative solutions are something to inspire me. In the future I learnt how the budget does not determine the quality.

## Nara, Deer Park

### Intent

The Nara Park is kinda popular and I heard of it before.

### Experience

There are indeed, many deer. I bought some deer biscuit but a single deer forcefully ripped it from my fingers. Additionally I visited the nearby shrines, and sat down on a bench and watched the deer just laze around.

### Memory

The sheer number of deer is certainly something to remember, though being robbed by a deer was not expected. Sitting on the bench was calming and relaxing.

### Insight

I guess I learnt how scary wild animals can be, and also how calming it is to do nothing. I wish to come back to stare at the deer in the future.

# Day 6 — Kyoto

<figure class="post-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/post-photos/2025-05-20-featured.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt=""><figcaption>Osaka, Japan</figcaption></figure>

## Kyoto, Fushimi Inari Shrine

### Intent

To be honest I was trying to avoid the crowd, I thought it would be cool since it would be late at night.

### Experience

I went down the shrine at 12AM basically, the entire place feels calming initially. However around the Tori gates the atmosphere becomes very oppressive, especially as I can hear the wild boars and friends. I did not finish the ascent as planned and I dipped out midway through.

### Memory

Something about the night turns a simple walk into a scary one. I think it is the lack of people or the darkness, however the number of Tori gates make the experience a surreal one.

### Insight

I hope to go again in the morning, though I learnt the importance of pulling out. The entire experience was a unique one but I am unsure if my heart can handle this.

## Kyoto, Railway Museum and Tower

### Intent

I didn't have any real goal in mind so I was just googling for interesting things.

### Experience/Memory

To be honest it is pretty forgettable, the only thing I remembered is that the fog blocked the view. Oh yes and there is a miniature section to drive trains. 

### Insights

Maybe spamming random activities and committing to it isn't the best idea if I clearly don't care about it.

## Kyoto, Gear Play

### Intent

I saw the advertisement as a "wordless" play, which is something I think I can enjoy even without a translator so I went inside.

### Experience

I sat down and we watched a play, the entire play features 5 characters and a set. The production is really high and there are flyers and stuff. I took a few home.

### Memory

This was the highlight of the Kyoto trip surprisingly, the entire play was something that conveyed emotion despite not a single word being spoken. They conveyed the sense of creativity and things quite well, as well as using creative stageplay. The emotions conveyed are really strong, I even bought the stageplay and some shirts. Even if it is all in Japanese so I need to get on with learning the language to read it.

### Insight

It seems I had some misconception on plays on account of only being exposed to my school's terrible plays. In the future I will seek out more theatres to watch and expand my horizon.

## Insight-Overall

I learnt the plan is to plan more time. 1 day in Kyoto is insufficient and one of the main issues I ran into is the fact that I am constrained by time, some attractions and museums are not open that day so I wasn't able to visit.
