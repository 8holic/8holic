# Foreword 
I had the chance to play 007 First Light from my friend's libary. It has been a while since I play a mainstream game.

# Summary
Overall it was a cinematic experience, and is enjoyable to look at. Gameplay wise I felt that the fist fight is enjoyable initally...the gameplay does not really change much from the first hour to the last hour. There is an absurd amount of handholding, get hit by a specifc attack and the game will pop up a menu to inform you of how to dodge said attack. 

This game is suitable for:
A casual player
A James Bond Fan

This game is not made for those:
looking for challanging gameplay
hoping for a open world
