# Day 1
As my introduction to Tallinn I arrived early in the morning when it is freezing. I will be staying close to 5 days here because well it is new year. So I took it relatively easy. For this day I only visited the Maritime Museum, I really enjoyed the exhibit. One of the exhibits simulate water pressure and it helped me appreciate the concept of barotrauma. Another segment involves piloting a boat which I was terrible at. To end the day off I got some KFC and rest in

# Day 2 New Years Eve
So for this day I saw a few really cute birds, hanging outside Viru. I end up heading to the Maritime Museum at Fat Margaret, and sat the boat I will be taking to head to Finland. I also briefly visited a prison museum and saw the inhumane situation that the dissenters were imprisoned in.

I did walk past a lot of pro-Russian protest, I was a little curious and it became clear when I checked my map and this is the Russian embassy. The Old Town area was quite beautiful and I saw quite a bit of buildings. Before new year I visited the Vabamu Museum of Occupations and Freedom, they showcase a lot of how life was like in various occupations, they even have an old Soviet drink dispenser that is still functional.

As the year came to a close I joined the Estonian New Year festival, I thought it was really cool even through I didn't really understand much, someone important I think the prime minister of Estonia appeared on screen to give a speech and everyone is cheering. This was my first year spending new years outside of Singapore.

# Day 3
Now obviously I didn't expect anything to be open so I got canned food ready, the two areas I visited were firstly the Nun Tower and Walls for a nice view, after that I visited a Cookie exhibition where they make art using cookies. Finally I headed to the Tallinn TV Tower to have an overview of the city, the view was nice even if it was really cold.
# Day 4
For day 2 I went to the open air museum and honestly it looks a little similar to the one in Riga. I did fall down when heading down the beach, now with the ocean frozen. It kinda hurts through the takeaway is probably do more research. The one segment that is unique to this one is the Soviet block, I enjoyed the exhibitions as they tell us how is life in each house. It felt like it as left as it instead of a curated museum piece, with an audio on how are people life here. Now since I am writing almost 8 months since then I don't remember much, but I need to come here again.
Before I left I also got some old buttons, it is quite nice. I got a Soviet Currency as well.
# Day 5
Today is the day I go to Finland, I think I was suffering from travel fatigue. I enjoyed the outside bar, well the bar is closed but it means I get to sleep there. The nice weather makes it comfortable to sleep in
