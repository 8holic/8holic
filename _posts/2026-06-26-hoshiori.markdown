# Common Route

## General Thoughts

I will first talk about the first half which is introducing everyone, and the second half which is used to establish the story.

One of the most surprising parts is that the protagonist, Ryosuke Hino...has hopes and dreams. I know very high bar to clear, however the sheer amount that is just a nice guy blank slate or a backstory that serves to justify them being overpowered means that this actually makes the protagonist interesting. We see the POV character is interested in Architecture, and this actually drives some of his actions later on. Most importantly, this means that I can actually see myself falling for him.

Afterwards we are introduced to the characters basically in this order, I think.

Sora Ousaka is the shy, quiet lass who loves astrology.

Natsuki Segawa is an energetic lass who loves photography.

Azuma Morio is a figure of admiration and mature despite being airheaded.

Marika Shinozaki is the sweet childhood friend who recovered from an illness.

Nirasawa Shuuichi who is a childhood friend with a friendly fierce competitive streak with the protagonist.

Rikka Narusawa is cold and stoic while being good at the piano.

Misa Okihara is a knowledgeable and sisterly lass about marine life.

Touko Yukimura who is a bit of a rebel.

I felt that the introductions did a good job at establishing who everyone is and the type of interest and personality they have. So I have no issue with the first segment at all.

For the second segment we are introduced to the Tanabata festival, and the town's unique celebration. This sets up the core driving force for most of the actions taken in the school segment.

For this segment I felt that it was alright, it did its job to make the introduced characters into a group. Narusawa-san is the only one that felt like she is literally dragged there by the party. This segment was not the most interesting since they still include more information for every character, and at this point I felt like the elaboration isn't really necessary. This entire segment is basically the "checkbox" to decide whose route you are romancing.

My biggest issue is that the choices do not feel like they matter. Early on Shinozaki-san has a choice to wait for her or go home, regardless of what you choose you still wait for her. It is probably used to count which part to put the player on but why not just...skip it. As a result I wasn't aware that these choices affect who we are pursuing later (since the outcome is exactly the same). I think it will be fine to skip it, so if we don't wait for Marika we skip the entire segment, which makes more sense into the romance part since well we spend less time with her.

The group scenes however are great, and the conversation at the end paints a good picture of what to expect from each girl when pursuing them. All the silly games they play do help in creating the atmosphere.

## Summary for the common route

I like the establishment of all the characters, as well as the story. I will appreciate if the game is not in fear that I will miss their brilliant writing.

# Sora Route

## General Thoughts - School Arc

So in this route we are basically stuck with Sora and assisting with the astrology club, who become talkative when it is about the stars. Notably Sora does demonstrate some interesting knowledge about astrology and the associated mythology. There are some light drama and issues they faced which are solved almost immediately, however the brief moments of setback are realistic and truly feel like life and death for that age.

The love confession and realization has quite a bit of build up, from when our protagonist realizes his feelings, to learning more about her then finally confessing at a chance. Notably the protagonist does not actually get a reply immediately and has to wait for her response. They continue per normal until she responds in the affirmative. The group of friends meanwhile play a supporting role offsetting Sora's shy nature, and continue to play a role through the festival contribution.

The next part can be broken down into two segments, but generally they face some problems and overcome them. The best way to describe it is that everyone works hard and it eventually worked out. It felt like they really cared for each other. Notably I was actually surprised at the third setback (since it has been so drama light). The finale felt like a suitable conclusion as we see their hard work pay off and Sora's dream is realized.

## General Thoughts - After Story

Whereas the school arc mainly focuses on realizing Sora's dream, there is a notable swap here where the protagonist takes center stage as he opens his own firm with Sora and best boss Azuma supporting him. Sora at this point has already mostly accomplished her goals in astrology which the protagonist gets to witness, the protagonist meanwhile faces some struggles maintaining his work life. My favorite scene is when the protagonist faints from overwork, and immediately continue trying to work again until Sora proposes to him. Where the terms of her proposal show she is willing to give up her own dream just to support him, causing the protagonist to realize how he has screwed up and take action to correct. Eventually he was able to fulfill his own dreams, and then he finally marries Sora and gaze at the stars together. The post credit scene confirms that Sora's dream is well and alive after the marriage as he and the other friends arrive to support Sora.

## General Thoughts - The Sex Scene

She has around 10 scenes I believe, and most of the scenes felt extremely similar. Basically I think the plot relevant ones are probably exactly 2. The first is used to show her more assertive side, and the second is used to show how she is willing to accept his fetishes as a part of him. Some of them do depict her as being more careful, however the usage of condom dies after the first raw experience so I am not sure if the above was intended.

## General Thoughts - Overall

This is a strong introduction to the novel, I like that it is relatively low stakes and used to further the characters. It has a strong element of mutually supportive relationship, and it helps that Sora is really cute throughout the route.

# Natsuki Route

## Background

The reason I decide to attempt to romance her is simply because she is the most prominent of all the love interests in Ousaka-san's path. From aiding in the love confession to showing up in the after story.

## General Thoughts - School Arc

Segawa-san School Arc difference is that she basically dragged the protagonist along and they eventually developed feelings. Her primary theme is photography. Her route is devoid of any major conflict and basically sees the protagonist keeping up with her as she takes photos for the club. Notably what the astrology and nature club end up doing both end up changing to make her a major player.

The love confession felt kinda abrupt. After spending enough time with each other, they realize they like one another. Or at least the protagonist seems to think so, however his obliviousness doesn't exactly convince me so. It does make use of photography that I find was neat, and is a very Segawa way of confessing.

Its primary focus is on her obsession with photography and the various things they did. The ending felt a little abrupt as they went to the festival and it ends... just like that I am a little sad that the group trip they were talking about just didn't happen. It might be better if that was never mentioned since I was expecting, you know, that group trip to be the climax of the story.

## General Thoughts - After Story

Natsuki (From here on) After Story places a heavy focus on the post marriage life, with a heavy emphasis on the ideal post marriage life. It is a very fluffy read and seems to cover the honeymoon period. The ending fits Natsuki Hino quite well and is photography relevant. They have their rituals, the people around them treating them after the marriage, and how they perform the chores and cook together when they are living as a couple. The story ends with the actual ceremony for the marriage conducted by the school.

## General Thoughts - The Sex Scene

She has around 9 scenes I believe, and somehow all of them are plot relevant as for a lack of better words showing them fucking raw with zero damn about the world leading to their shotgun marriage is perhaps the most realistic part about fucking like rabbits. Even if they discover they have no child it is highly believable. The most plot relevant one is when they are both late due to fucking deep at night. The rest is passable by explaining how they can believe in the pregnancy.

## General Thoughts - Overall

Segawa route is rather unique due to her airheadedness, and unique personality. It is a fluffy story that somehow doesn't stick in my mind. I am optimistic that she might not be my type.
