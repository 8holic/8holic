# Overview
## Rome
Well at this point I think I have already knocked out all the famous big destinations I am interested in, so I spent the day just wandering around rome. Among the destinations wa the Trevi Fountain. I end up visiting the Mercadti Di traniano Museo Dei Fori Imperiali, and in english is the Trajan Markets. This location was suprising quiet and I felt like I was transported back into history. Especially when walking the streets where you physically cannot see and hear the rest of rome.

I also wandered around a bit and end up at Circus Maximus, I was kinda suprised I missed this considering its proximity to the Colossum which I literally just went 2 days ago. But it was nice, the VR Store closed so I just sat around and imagine how it was used.

I wandered around and walk through one of those car tunnels, also ate some nice pasta. I bought MacDonalds back to the hotel to call it a night.
