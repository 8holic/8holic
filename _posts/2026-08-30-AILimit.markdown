# Progression: The unlimited respec item, yay
So I was exploring for more weapons and stuff when I come across the infinite Respec crystal, in contrast to other similar games this game has infinite respec which is a dream come true. I felt like more games should just do this or do away with stats altogether. Thanks to this I was able to try all sorts of weird weapons like a literal road sign, and some of the high stat spells. Thanks to this I was able to explore quite the build variety and improve my damage.

# Hunter of Bladers
Dying to her the first time gets you locked up in jail, kinda cool. Though the color palette is rather similar.
Death:54
The most notable thing is this boss has her own sync meter as well making this the first official duel boss, where she feels like the player character. I suspect that she is the flashback we see earlier, but it was a really challenging and fun fight. 
She is capable of parrying the player, and her attacks are fast but readable. I suspect that I could have beaten her a lot faster if I don't insist on parrying her as she kills me in 2-3 hits. Compared to other bosses the parrying return isn't as great as well, because if she hits the player or parries the player she regains a good chunk of her sync bar, I think each parry drops it by 35%, so to bring her down to the first fatal attack it takes 3 or so parries, and she is at 50% sync meter necessitating 1-2 parries. There wasn't any point where I felt that I died unfairly, and I was able to defeat her at the end. When she is near death she does start using items to restore her sync, so I find no point in parrying from here on.
