# Overview
So as usual i arrived in the early morning and checked in. I thought the city was beautiful as I walk through the riverside. It was also snowing.

I went to Lido for a meal and it was relatively nice. It remind me of the "Caifan" from my homeland.

I felt that the sight at the river was really beautiful. For the first time I also went for a spa 

# The Next Day
I went to the Ethnographic Open-Air Museum of Latvia. It has a lot of old houses and I learnt a bit about the ancient technqiues. I was also jumpscared by a cat from one of the houses, so there was that. Still walking around in the snow was an unique experience. It felt like travelling through history. Even through most are closed the place looks stunning in winter, I learnt a bit about how life was at Lativa a long time ago.

As I went back to have another meal at Lido, I felt that I didn't spend enough time here. However this short time is memoriable. Walking across the city in snow was really nice.
