# Day Trip
## Avila
So this is my day trip to Avila, the city known for its town walls. I thought that the place is well preserved, through unknowingly I went on renfair, so it was a lot more crowded than usual. It is here where I realized that Spanish Museums seems to be a lot more localized, Avila was a pretty cool trip through, I slowly walked the castle walls and observe the city. 

## RenFair
To be honest I am not famillar enough to fully take advantage of the event, however walking through the booth feels a little like walking through history. I saw dragons and knights, as well as archery booth. I think I made a few realization here, firstly being that English is not really spoken outside of Madrid. So if I want to continue visiting small towns, I need to start learning some Spanish. I did have some nice meat, it is pretty damn good.

## Avila Castle Walls
Walking upon what Avila is known for is both a magical and tiring experience. Most of the highlight comes from the scenery from the walls. Seeing the rivers from this vantage point feels special especially as I walk the half crumbling stairs that I almost did not want to leave at the end. I also saw what I thought was Spencer Mansion(I found out later it is probably the Seminary). For the price it is absolutely worth it.

## Mirador entrada Ávila
Since it is kinda hard to look at Avila walls on them, I took a trip to a nice vantage point, I was worried I wont arrive in time for the return train and it end up being a close call. Seeing Avila from a distance away the walls looks beautiful guarding the city. It is amazing how long it survived
