# Day 1 - CHRISTMAS!
I arrived in the morning at Warsaw and the difference was immediate, as I arrived in the morning I expected everything to be closed. So I was a lot more relaxed. For the entire day I walked around admiring the building. To be honest overall it was a nice and relaxing experience, just what I needed after my intense times in the exams.
I mostly admired the various building, There were also some expressions of art that I saw, I found a graffiti at the old war equating Hilter to Putin. At night the palace of culture and science came ot light in a gree. As well as the christmas market. I gotta say the Christmas market felt amazing, the food was really good even if I felt that I got scammed by the pricing. One of the store states their prices as per 100g instead, so the actual thing was 5 times the price.

For this museum I visited the polish uprising museum, this was where I actually learn of how most of Warsaw was razed by hilter as reprisal and much of what i saw was faithfully recreated. Yet it doesnt feel out of place, the great effort that went into it cannot be underestimated.
