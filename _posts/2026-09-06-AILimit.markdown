# DLC rogeulite 
So I cleared a good part of the DLC up to the second time up to Eirene boss fight. I was extremely suprised we have a roguelite mode in well a soulslike, but I guess Elden Ring also did it. 

How it works is that there is a initial linear stage, selected randomly between two different layout. The enemies are usually the same with the exception of a few rooms that has 2 choices. After this segment, there are a few maps of psuedo open world where the player can choose the order to tackle the enemies. Each zone have a symbol showing the specific rewards they gave.

For an roguelite I find that it is suprising simillar, the only random element is what the game decide to bestow upon the user. I eventurally memorised the entire layout.  There were a few bosses through they are optional.

For the gameplay...I felt like with RNG on your side the roguelite suddendly become zero skill. There were a few runs where I got rewards that only activate after a skilled play(for example parrying giving armor), and I get absolutely destroyed by anyone I can't fight reliably. On my winning run the stars align and I got a special attack that activte with charged attacks,complete with a speed up charging and weapon swapping triggers spells while attacking with spells trigger a follow up, and I nuked everyone so hard that I barely had to dodge anything. I guess this is a function of the roguelite, through it feels a little sad that I am beating these cool new bossses by virtue of being unkillable.

In regards to the other game mode which I am kinda confused about, they introduced a boss rush and boss rematch mode, however some bosses are just kinda missing. Including the final bosses as well as some of the secret bosses in the roguelite mode which I find confusing, I will like to fight them with my regular kit in the future.

For a free DLC it is pretty damn good...but becomes repetitive quickly as I memorise everything. Not that I mind through because the gameplay is relatively fun and I get to try out all the weird weapon I never had a chance to do so in the campaign. This is perhaps the one saving grance(even if I felt like quite a few weapons aren't present).
